06 / Good questions

Before we
press record.

Something else on your mind? Bring it to the call or send me an email.

Do I have to be on camera?

It helps people get to know your business, but it's your choice. We can film your work, your team, your process or your space. If you do speak on camera, I guide what to say and keep the session relaxed.

How much of my time will this take?

Plan for the shoot time in your package, a short planning conversation and batch approvals. I handle the concepts, scripts, filming, editing and posting. We confirm the schedule around your business before you sign.

What happens on the free call?

We spend 30 minutes on your business, your audience and what your content needs to do. I'll review your profile and suggest a starting point. You can decide afterward whether a starter project or monthly plan makes sense.

Can I start without a monthly commitment?

Yes. The $750 CAD starter is a separate, one-time project with four short-form videos and one shoot of up to two hours. Monthly plans begin with a three-month term, then continue month to month. Applicable tax is extra.

What do you measure?

Video reach and views, profile visits and bio or booking-link clicks, where the platforms make them available. These help us review how content moves people toward your offer. Bookings also depend on your offer, booking process and follow-up, so I don't promise a set number of customers.

Who is this a good fit for?

Toronto and GTA businesses with work, expertise or an experience worth showing: studios, gyms, service businesses, trades, shops and more. The strongest fit is a business that can join scheduled shoots, approve content and handle incoming inquiries.

Are events, ads or longer videos included?

Monthly plans focus on short-form content. Event coverage, longer brand films, paid ad management, extra locations and DM automation can be scoped separately. Tell me what you need and I'll put the scope and price in writing.
