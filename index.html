What happens on the call?
What counts as one video?

One finished short-form video, up to 60 seconds. If it’s posted to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, it still counts as one video.

Who posts the videos?

On monthly plans, I do. Content Engine posts to Instagram; Growth System and Performance post to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. With the Content Kickstart, you post them using the posting guide I give you. You reply to your own DMs and comments.

What if I’m uncomfortable on camera?

We can build videos around your work, your process, your space or your team. If you do speak, I help with the words and guide every take. You won’t be left to improvise.

What if I need more filming time?

Add an extra 2-hour filming block in Toronto or the GTA from . Multi-location shoots, events and long sessions are quoted before anything is booked, so there are no surprise charges.

What if I don’t like an edit?

Every delivery batch includes one round of revisions. And before filming, we agree in writing on the creative direction. If your first batch doesn’t match it, I’ll revise or reshoot those videos at no charge.

How do payments work?

Content Kickstart: to book and before you receive the final files. Monthly plans are paid before each month’s planning begins. ManyChat and ad spend are paid separately, straight to those platforms. All prices are in CAD, plus applicable tax.

Can I cancel?

Content Engine and Growth System are month to month. Give 14 days’ notice before your next month and it stops. Performance has a 3-month minimum because ads need time to test. The Content Kickstart is one project with no ongoing commitment.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. I guarantee the work: what we agree on in writing is what you get. Views, followers and sales also depend on your offer, pricing, website and how you follow up with people. I’ll show you what’s working each month and what we’re changing.
