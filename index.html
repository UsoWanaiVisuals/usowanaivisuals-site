How It Works

Simple.

01

Send the Brief

Tell me what it's for, where it is, and when you need it. I come back with my creative direction — the look, the shots, how it gets cut — and we lock the final plan together before anything is booked.

02

Shoot Day

I show up with the FX3, lighting, and drone where it's needed. We're working off the plan we agreed on, so nothing on the day is a surprise.

03

Delivery

Colour-graded cuts sized for wherever they're going — landscape for the site, vertical for Reels and TikTok. Two rounds of revisions included.
